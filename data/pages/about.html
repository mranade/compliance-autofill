Moving the world's freight, one shipment at a time

Halden Logistics builds shipment-tracking and freight-booking software for mid-sized manufacturers and retailers. More than 1,200 companies use Halden to book carriers, track containers, and share delivery updates with their customers.

Where we are

Halden Logistics B.V. is headquartered in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, with additional offices in Berlin, Germany and Chicago, United States.

Our story

Founded in 2016 by two former port operations managers, Halden started as a spreadsheet shared between a handful of shippers. In 2023 we began our SOC 2 journey, and we have grown to 340 employees across three countries.

Why customers trust us

Bank-grade security you can count on. Your data is always safe with Halden, and we never compromise on protection.
