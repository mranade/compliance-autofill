Security and trust at Halden

Protecting our customers' shipment data is at the heart of everything we do. Here is how we keep it safe.

Certifications

Halden has completed a SOC 2 Type II audit covering the period January 1 to December 31, 2025, performed by an independent auditor. Customers and prospects can request the full report under a non-disclosure agreement.

Encryption

All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.

Data retention

We retain customer shipment data for 24 months after a customer's contract ends. After that period, the data is permanently deleted from our production systems and backups.

Incident response

If we confirm a security breach affecting customer data, we notify affected customers within 72 hours of confirmation.

Our partners

We work with carefully selected, industry-leading cloud and infrastructure partners who share our commitment to security.

Report a vulnerability

Found a security issue? Email our security team at security@halden.example. We aim to respond within two business days.
